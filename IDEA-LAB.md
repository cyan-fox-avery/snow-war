# Snow War — Idea Lab

A day-by-day tactical schoolyard snowball war across one Canadian winter.
A love letter to growing up Canadian (Ontario/Québec, early-to-mid 2000s, elementary school).
A for-himself game, in the Rockhound family.

## The pitch

Risk / Age of Empires, but entirely at recess. Territory control across a schoolyard,
one winter long. Ends when the snow melts.

## The winter (authored, not random)

A pre-built season with timed events and predetermined weather — scored like music:

- **First Snow** (late Nov): electric. Everyone's out, forts go up overnight.
- **Deep Freeze** (Jan): the grind. −30 mornings, indoor recess days, bus cancellations over the PA.
- **The False Spring** (Feb): a thaw tease, then the betrayal blizzard. Roll Up the Rim begins.
- **The Melt** (Mar): urgent. Slush, shrinking forts, everyone knows it's ending. Last chances.

## Winning: reputation, emergent styles

No hard-coded classes or builds. Play styles emerge from behavior — the game recognizes
your style and scores quiet victory thresholds per style. Reputation is earned
differently per style. End-of-winter breakdown: *"70% diplomat, 11% ghost"* — the game
hands you a mirror.

Archetypes seen so far: Blitz, Fortress, Cannon, Diplomat, Ghost, Quartermaster,
Trickster (+ more TBD). Hybrids get a mixed legend.

## The playground code

No ice balls. No purposely destroying forts. Respect the littles.
Guardians (recess monitors) enforce it: whistle + warning → benched (miss a day) →
the office. Dirty play is possible but never rewarded — the game prefers fairness
mechanically, the way our other games prefer kindness.

## The map

- **The Big Pile** (plough-made): prime fort real estate, scarce base spots; summit forbidden (instant whistle)
- **The Hill**: the other high ground
- **The Trees**: cover — ambush country
- **The Creek Bed**: the divot — cover that foreshadows the melt
- **The Soccer Field**: the open battlefield
- **The Fences**: long defensive lines around the edges
- **The Playground**: demilitarized safe zone — the littles' territory

## Nostalgia texture

Roll Up the Rim (the teachers'/parents' coffee — it's elementary!), Silly Bandz
(diplomacy currency), Heelys (mobility edge), milk bags at lunch, the VCR cart on
indoor days, the lost-and-found mountain of single mitts, PA announcements,
Christmas concert chaos. Period playlist research underway (Canadian radio 2002–2009).

For those who lived it: immersion in memory. For everyone else: a window into
someone else's childhood.
